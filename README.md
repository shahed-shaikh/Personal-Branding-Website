FREELANCE is an independent freelancer portfolio built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and custom animations. Its visual style combines a light background, bold typography, and blue accents.

The hero introduces Jordan Blake, highlighting his experience, completed projects, availability, and creative approach. A trusted brands strip leads into four works across brand identity, website design, creative direction, and digital strategy.

The expertise section presents Website Design, Brand Development, Graphic Design, and Digital Consulting. The about section shares Jordan's independent working style, values, and approach to remote collaboration.

Client testimonial adds a personal perspective, while the contact section invites new project enquiries. The footer includes navigation, information links, social destinations, and email contact.

Built with Tailwind CSS, responsive grids, animated hero content, image reveals, floating elements, orbit animation, image zoom, hover transitions, and reduced-motion support, FREELANCE presents a professional portfolio for an independent creative partner.
